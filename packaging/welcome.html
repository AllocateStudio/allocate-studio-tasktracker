Step 2 (optional). Want an offline copy instead? Unzip the download and open index.html. Keep every file and folder together exactly as extracted.

Getting started

1

The tracker starts empty. Want to look around first? In Settings, click Add samples for 20 example tasks, then Remove samples when you’re done.

2

Click + Add task. Give it a title and a due date, then pick a project, priority and status. Notes are optional.

3

Need it again and again? Set Repeat to Daily, Weekdays, Weekly or Monthly. Tick it off and the next one appears on its own.

4

Start each day with the Today card: today’s tasks plus anything overdue. Tick them off, or use the calendar icon to move a task to tomorrow, next week or any date.

5

Open Calendar to see your month or week at a glance. In Settings, add or rename your projects and choose whether your week starts on Sunday or Monday.

One thing worth knowing: your tasks live only in this browser, not in the cloud. Once you’ve added your own tasks, go to Settings, click Export backup and keep the file somewhere safe.

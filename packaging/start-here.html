Start here: how your tasks are saved

Welcome! Before you add your real tasks, there’s one important thing to understand about how the app works. It only takes two minutes to read, and it will save you a headache later.

Your tasks live in your browser, not on any server. The app doesn’t send your tasks to us or to anyone else. But that also means it’s your job to keep a backup, the same way you’d back up any file that only exists in one place. This guide shows you exactly how.

The one big idea

Think of it like writing in a notebook. The notebook (this app) is something you can open again and again. But everything you write in it (your tasks, projects, dates and notes) is saved by your web browser, on the one device you’re using right now. That means:

  • Open it on your phone and on your laptop, and you’ll see two different notebooks. They don’t talk to each other.
  • Clear this site’s data, and the notebook’s pages go blank.
  • Switch from Chrome to Safari, even on the same computer, and it’s a brand new notebook again.

None of this is a bug. It’s simply how the app keeps your tasks private.

Getting started

Open your task tracker online: https://allocatestudio.github.io/allocate-studio-tasktracker/. It needs an internet connection to load.

Prefer an offline copy on your computer? Unzip the file you downloaded and open index.html in that folder. Keep every file and folder together exactly as extracted. Don’t delete, rename or move anything individually.

Either way, the online version and a local copy don’t share data: they’re two separate notebooks. To move tasks between them, use Export backup on one and Import backup on the other, same as moving to any other device.

Save your first backup (do this today)

30 seconds
  1. Go to Settings and click Export backup in the Backup & restore section.
  2. Check your Downloads folder and make sure the file is actually there. Don’t just assume it worked.
  3. Keep it somewhere you’ll remember: a folder on your computer, an external drive, or your own cloud storage. It’s your personal to-do list, so don’t post it anywhere public.

Add it to your home screen

Open your task tracker like an app, with one tap. It gets its own green Tasks icon.

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow). On newer iPhones, tap ••• first, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep Open as Web App switched on, then tap Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then Add (or Install).

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

Bookmark the page, or open the ⋮ menu and choose Cast, save and share → Install page as app (in Edge: Apps → Install this site as an app).

Important: add the icon first, then add your tasks. On iPhone, the home screen app keeps its own data, separate from Safari. If you’ve already started in Safari, click Export backup there, then open the home screen app and click Import backup.

When to make a new backup

Basically: whenever you’ve made changes you’d be annoyed to lose. As a simple habit, export a fresh backup:

  • Once a week if you’re using the app regularly
  • Right before you clear this site’s data
  • Right before switching to a new phone, computer or browser
  • Right before importing an older backup

The app never does this automatically. It’s always your click.

Getting your tasks back (restoring a backup)

  1. Go to Settings and click Import backup in the Backup & restore section.
  2. Choose the backup file you saved earlier, the file the app itself created when you clicked Export backup.
  3. The app asks you to confirm, because the backup replaces every task currently in the app. If there’s anything there you want to keep, click Export backup first.
  4. After it’s done, take a quick look and check that a few tasks and dates look right.

Using the app on more than one device

Your tasks don’t follow you between devices on their own. To move them:

  1. On your old device, click Export backup.
  2. Open the app on the new device.
  3. Click Import backup there and choose that file.

From that point on, pick one device as your main one. If you keep adding tasks on both, they’ll quietly drift apart, and you’ll end up with two different lists.

Can’t find your tasks?

Before panicking, check the obvious things first:

  • Are you in the exact same browser and same profile you used before?
  • Did you add the app to your iPhone home screen after starting in Safari? Your tasks are still in Safari. Export them there and import them in the home screen app.
  • Private/incognito windows don’t save anything after you close them. Were you in one?
  • Did something get cleared (a “clean up my computer” tool, a browser reset)?

If your tasks really are gone and there’s no backup, they can’t be recovered. They were only ever on your device, so we don’t have a copy either.

Something looks off?

Top right, the app normally says Saved on this device. If it says Not saved. Export a backup or Storage unavailable. Export a backup, your browser isn’t keeping your changes.

Click Export backup in Settings right away, so nothing you entered is lost. Then allow site data for this page or leave the private window, reload, and use Import backup if anything is missing.

Two tabs open at once? Use one tab at a time. Open tabs don’t update each other, so the tab you change last wins and changes made in the other tab can be lost.

Test your backup once, so you trust it

Right after your first backup, open the app in a different browser (or profile) and import that file there. Check that a few tasks match, then go back to your regular browser. Now you know restoring works before you ever need it.
